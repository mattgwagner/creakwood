How to read this file:

- **✔** marks the right option. The game shuffles the order.
- **Part B** is the evidence tap: the player taps the sentence in the passage that proves the Part A answer. The accepted sentence(s) are quoted.
- **Hint** is what Wick says after a wrong first answer. A second wrong answer shows the right answer with the proving sentence lit up.
- **Extra** questions are only asked when the player missed that skill earlier in the case (one per clue, if available).
- __Underlined__ words in passages are the vocabulary words.
